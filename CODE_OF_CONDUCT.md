# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming project, we pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Showing empathy toward other community members
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive comments
- Personal attacks or insults
- Disruptive or aggressive behavior
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct.

## Reporting

If you are being harassed or notice a violation of this code of conduct, please contact the repository maintainers.
